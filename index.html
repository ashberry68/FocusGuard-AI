FocusGuard · on-device prototype

Your focus, measured on your machine only

This demo estimates presence and attention from your webcam and tab activity — entirely inside this browser tab.

  • Video frames are analyzed locally and immediately discarded — none are recorded or uploaded
  • Only a rolling focus score and event log are kept, and only in this tab's memory
  • No network requests are made once the page loads — you can verify this in your browser's network tab
  • Closing this tab erases everything
